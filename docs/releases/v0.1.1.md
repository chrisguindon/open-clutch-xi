# Open Clutch XI 0.1.1 (public beta)

A small update to the [0.1.0 public beta](v0.1.0.md) that makes the game easier to play on phones. There's still no download, account or tracking. See the [README](../../README.md) for what the game is and how to contribute.

## Fixed

- **Player names no longer hide the action on phones.** On touch screens and small windows, your player's name now appears over the pitch briefly after the pre-match intro and when you switch players, then fades. The ring at your player's feet and the player card in the corner show who you control. Desktop keeps the name above your player (see [controls](../gameplay/controls.md)).
- **A clear rotate screen on phones.** Held upright, a phone now shows a full-screen *Turn your phone sideways* screen instead of a small pop-up that let the match carry on underneath. The match pauses while the phone is upright and resumes when you turn it back, unless you had paused it yourself. Tablets and desktops are never blocked.
- **The player card is readable on phones** (it was shrunk to 70%), and the scoreboard, player card and minimap keep clear of notches and rounded corners in landscape.
- **Links in the GitHub release notes** now point to the documents at the release's tag, and every link is checked before a release is published.

The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Saved data

Nothing changes: settings and records saved by 0.1.0 carry over as they are.

## Known limitations

- **Portrait isn't supported on phones:** play is landscape only.
- **Not tested on real devices:** the phone changes (rotation, safe areas around notches, the brief player name) were checked in browser emulation only. iOS Safari, Android Chrome, orientation locks, split-screen and foldable phones still need checking by people.
- Everything listed for 0.1.0 still applies. More: [known issues](../development/known-issues.md).

## Testing status

- **Automated (local):** unit, scenario and simulation tests; headless-browser flows, including new checks for the player name and HUD layout in phone, tablet and desktop layouts and for the rotate screen while an emulated phone turns; licence, SBOM, privacy, repository-hygiene and documentation checks.
- **Not yet done:** human playtesting on real phones (see the [playtesting guide](../development/playtesting.md)).
