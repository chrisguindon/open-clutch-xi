# Open Clutch XI 0.1.0 (public beta) — draft

> **Draft.** These notes are edited as development continues. Nothing has been released or published. The maintainer decides when to tag and publish v0.1.0.

Open Clutch XI is a free, open-source 3D football game for the browser: pick a national team and play against the computer. No download, no account, no tracking. Play at https://fc.chrisguindon.com.

## Highlights

- **Play:** quick matches (3v3, 5v5, 11v11), a penalty shootout, a practice pitch and an interactive tutorial. 54 national teams, shown with flags and national colours only, as women's, men's or mixed squads.
- **Football rules:** 11v11 uses **Standard** rules: offside, fouls with yellow and red cards, advantage, assistant referees, walls at free kicks, and the five-second throw-in countdown. 3v3 and 5v5 use faster **Arcade** rules. Restarts follow the Laws: you can't carry the ball at a throw-in, opponents keep their distance, and a second touch is penalised. See the [rules matrix](../gameplay/FOOTBALL_RULES.md).
- **Referees:** a referee and (in 11v11) assistant referees who follow play and signal every decision. Decisions are also shown in words, and announced to screen readers.
- **Difficulty:** Beginner, Easy, Medium and Hard. Difficulty changes how the computer thinks, never how fast it runs. Both teams use the same formation.
- **Celebrations:** varied, chosen from the moment (a late winner, a consolation goal, an own goal), and always skippable.
- **Controls:** keyboard, keyboard and mouse, controller and touch, all remappable. A first-launch "How would you like to play?" screen.
- **Graphics:** Auto adapts resolution, effects and frame rate to your device, from 30 fps up to your display's refresh rate.
- **Open source:** EPL-2.0. A learning guide, starter issues, CI quality gates and an SBOM.

## Known limitations

- No half-time, extra time or substitutions. Offside and foul detection are simplified (see the [rules matrix](../gameplay/FOOTBALL_RULES.md)).
- All players currently share one base body. A female base body is planned.
- 11v11 AI-vs-AI play creates few chances, and AI crosses rarely succeed.
- **Not yet tested by people on real devices:** phones, tablets, controllers, fullscreen and high-refresh monitors. Automated checks use emulation and software rendering.
- More: [known issues](../development/known-issues.md).

## Testing status

- **Automated:** unit, scenario and whole-match simulation tests, including Laws of the Game regression tests; headless-browser gameplay flows (restarts, celebrations, controls, adaptive quality, tutorial, production build); licence, SBOM, privacy and documentation checks. See [testing](../development/testing.md).
- **Not yet done:** human playtesting (see the [playtesting guide](../development/playtesting.md)) and GitHub-hosted CI runs.
