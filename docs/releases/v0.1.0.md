# Open Clutch XI 0.1.0 (public beta): draft release notes

> **Draft.** Nothing has been tagged, released or published. These notes describe the local release candidate. The maintainer decides when to tag and publish v0.1.0.

Open Clutch XI is a free, open-source 3D football game for the browser: pick a national team and play against the computer. There's no download, account or tracking. It's also a learning project. Most of its code was written with AI assistance and reviewed by a person, and contributors are invited to explore it, find what's wrong and improve it (see the [README](../../README.md)).

## Highlights

- **Play:** quick matches (3v3, 5v5, 11v11), the **XI Nations Cup** (a fictional tournament, Short or Classic), a penalty shootout, a practice pitch and an interactive tutorial. 54 national teams, shown with flags and national colours only, as women's, men's or mixed squads.
- **Football rules:** 11v11 uses **Standard** rules: offside, fouls with cards, advantage, assistant referees, walls, and the throw-in countdown. 3v3 and 5v5 use faster **Arcade** rules. Every restart follows the Laws. Simplifications are listed in the [rules matrix](../gameplay/FOOTBALL_RULES.md).
- **AI:** utility-based decisions you can watch live (F3). Difficulty changes how the computer thinks, never how fast it runs.
- **Movement and animation:** leg tempo matched to stride length, so feet don't skate. Players lean into bursts, stops and turns, and goalkeepers hold a ready stance. Celebrations are chosen from the moment and always skippable.
- **Records:** statistics, recent matches and 12 achievements, saved only in your browser, with export, import and reset.
- **Controls and accessibility:** keyboard, keyboard and mouse, controller and touch, all remappable. Decisions are shown in words, reduce motion is available, and graphics adapt to the device.
- **Hosting:** a static web app with no backend. The release includes a ready-to-host web archive and the recommended security headers. Any site can add its own title, link previews, board panels and creator portrait without rebuilding (see [hosting](../development/hosting.md)).

## Saved data

New installations start fresh. Settings and records saved by development builds are kept and migrated. The XI Nations Cup adds a `tournament-v1` record. **Going back** to a build from before the cup achievements isn't supported: older builds keep a newer record as a recovery copy and start afresh (see [known issues](../development/known-issues.md)).

## Known limitations

- No half-time or extra time: a level XI Nations Cup knockout goes straight to penalties, a simplification the game states on screen. No substitutions. Offside and foul judgements are simplified.
- 11v11 still creates far fewer chances than small-sided play, and AI crosses reach a team-mate only some of the time.
- Animations, celebrations and goalkeeper poses have been checked with automated captures, not yet by people watching them move.
- **Not tested on real devices:** phones, tablets, controllers, fullscreen and high-refresh monitors. Automated checks use emulation and software rendering.
- More: [known issues](../development/known-issues.md).

## Testing status

- **Automated (local):**
  - unit, scenario and whole-match simulation tests, including Laws of the Game regression tests;
  - headless-browser flows: menus and a full match, restarts, controls, the tutorial with deliberate mistakes, a whole XI Nations Cup, phone emulation, adaptive quality, and the production build under the security headers;
  - licence, SBOM, privacy, repository-hygiene and documentation checks.
- **Not yet done:** human playtesting (see the [playtesting guide](../development/playtesting.md)) and CI runs on GitHub.
